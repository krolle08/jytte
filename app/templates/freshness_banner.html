{# F5 freshness/error banner. Renders a tight one-line banner at the top of any widget card that uses the F5 substrate. Reads from the `state` dict passed to every card render (`state.last_success_at`, `state.consecutive_failures`, `state.last_error`). The `data-ts` attribute carries the raw ISO 8601 UTC timestamp; the inline script at the bottom rewrites the visible text to the browser's local timezone + a relative "N min ago" tail. Falls back to the server-rendered text if JS is disabled. {% endif %}
