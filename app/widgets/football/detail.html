Reading this

P = calibrated probability (Platt-scaled, what we display). ensemble μ = mean across 20 bootstrap models. ensemble σ = standard deviation. Higher σ means the individual models disagree more about this match - signal that the model is extrapolating outside its comfort zone.

contribution = standardized feature value × logistic-regression coefficient for the predicted class. Linear in standardized space, so the sign tells you whether the feature pushed the model toward or away from the prediction.
